A gift can be beautiful because of what it makes room for.

An afternoon to rest. A dinner someone else has handled. A child getting help with a difficult subject. A household task that finally stops following someone from one to-do list to the next.

Is that as sexy as a Chanel bag under the tree? Probably not. But on the right Tuesday, it may be the gift someone appreciates most.

## Eight gifts of help, with clear boundaries

- **Two dinners:** offer two menu choices, check dietary needs and include delivery and suitable storage instructions.
- **An afternoon of childcare:** agree on caregiver, location, date and the parent's instructions first.
- **Two hours of household help:** let the recipient choose laundry, organization or another manageable task; do not diagnose their house for them.
- **Four tutoring sessions:** name the subject and session length, and offer only subjects you can competently teach.
- **One tech-help afternoon:** organize photos or explain phone settings with the owner present. Do not ask them to hand over passwords.
- **A skill-sharing session:** baking, mending, drawing or another skill you can teach; include materials or state who supplies them.
- **A holiday cleanup shift:** pack décor or wash dishes on an agreed date, using the recipient's system.
- **An errand together:** offer transport or pickup only when the arrangements and costs are clear.

## Copy this gift card

> **A Little Breathing Room**
>
> My gift to you: ____
>
> Included: ____
>
> Time or number of sessions: ____
>
> I will contact you by ____ with three possible dates.
>
> If another kind of help would be more useful, we can choose together.
>
> With love, ____

This is a personal promise, not a Place & Plenty voucher or a paid service booking.

## Make the gift easier to receive

Ask privately before promising something personal. Offer two choices instead of a vague “anything.” Do the scheduling follow-up yourself. Keep the commitment realistic, including your own childcare, travel and supplies. If circumstances change, contact the recipient and agree on an alternative rather than disappearing.
