Before the season reaches full speed, picture the January you would like to have.

Maybe there is room to rest. Maybe the decorations are packed where you can find them, the calendar is quieter, and the spending stayed within a range you felt comfortable with. A few decisions now can make that version of January easier to reach.

Leave some of your holiday budget unassigned. It gives you room for the unexpected invitation or the ingredient you forgot without making every surprise a crisis. If you do not need it, it does not have to become a last-minute purchase.

## A 15-minute holiday closeout

1. **Five minutes for spending:** record remaining commitments and purchases; cancel only items you no longer need and can legitimately cancel. Do not spend an unused allowance just to use it up.
2. **Five minutes for belongings:** list borrowed items and return dates. Separate repairs from decorations that are ready to pack.
3. **Five minutes for next time:** write one thing to keep, one to change and one to skip.

## Your pack-away kit

- Existing storage boxes and reusable padding.
- Labels or tape and a pen.
- Small bags for hooks, spare parts and ribbon.
- A repair note for anything that needs attention.
- A photo of the arrangement you want to repeat.

Store items according to their care instructions. Keep electrical decorations and batteries as their manufacturers recommend; a packing shortcut should not damage what you planned to reuse.

## The keep, change, skip worksheet

- **Keep:** the dish, tradition or purchase that earned its place.
- **Change:** one task to share earlier or one quantity to adjust.
- **Skip:** something expensive or tiring that nobody missed.
- **Buy next time only if needed:** a specific item and quantity.
- **Ask earlier next year:** one person or decision that needs more lead time.

Invite household members to contribute. Their favorite part may have been the simplest, but ask rather than deciding for them.

## Share the final work

Give helpers a specific task: clear and wipe one surface, wash serving pieces, label one box, or gather borrowed items for return. Agree on a stopping point. Leave an evening free after the busiest gathering if that would help your household recover.
