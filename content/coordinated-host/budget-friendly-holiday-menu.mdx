The instinct when people are coming over is often to add another dish. Another side, another dessert, something extra in case the first extra was not enough.

Every addition brings ingredients, preparation, serving space, and cleanup. A generous table can begin with a smaller, more deliberate menu.

First, decide what kind of gathering you are hosting. Brunch, soup and bread, dessert and coffee, or a traditional dinner can all feel complete when the invitation makes the plan clear. Guests should know whether they are arriving for a meal or something lighter.

## Three complete menus to price locally

- **Soup night:** a hearty soup, a second option for dietary needs, bread, simple salad and one dessert.
- **Holiday brunch:** egg bake or another suitable main, potatoes, fruit, one baked treat, coffee and water.
- **Dessert and coffee:** two desserts with a suitable option for dietary needs, coffee, tea and water. Say “dessert and coffee” in the invitation so people do not arrive expecting dinner.

Choose one format. These are menu structures, not price guarantees. Check recipes for yield and price your actual ingredient quantities before committing.

## Build the shopping list in this order

1. Confirm adults, children and dietary needs without assuming every child eats the same amount.
2. Record how many servings each recipe makes and how many batches you need.
3. Subtract dishes guests have confirmed they will bring.
4. Check pantry and freezer quantities, including whether an open package is enough.
5. Add drinks, ice if needed, serving supplies and take-home containers.
6. Compare your basket total with the limit; simplify the menu before adding another trip to another store.

## A $120 planning example for 12 people

- Main: $45
- Sides and bread: $30
- Dessert: $15
- Drinks: $15
- Tax and unexpected costs: $15
- Total: $120, or $10 per person

These are spending allowances. If local prices or dietary needs do not fit, change the menu or contribution plan. Do not treat the example as a promise to feed every group for that amount.
