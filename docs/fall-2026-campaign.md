## MailerLite journey to configure after account access is available

Keep marketing consent optional and unchecked. Checklist-only recipients get the
immediate website download; do not enroll them in promotional email sequences.
For opted-in subscribers, use occasion groups and one deduplicated welcome journey.

1. Immediately: "Your [occasion] checklist is here". Link directly to the matching
   PDF, then an occasion-preserving account CTA. Do not claim this email is live.
2. Two days later: one helpful tip from that edition (Friendsgiving: assign categories
   before dishes; bachelorette: agree on budget; reunion: one contact per household).
3. Five days later: a short real product walkthrough showing setup through invitation
   preview. Invite them to start their own gathering; no fabricated testimonials.

Each marketing email needs the account's normal unsubscribe and sender details.
Stop account-creation prompts for existing users only after a reliable account
lifecycle integration exists. Group membership by itself cannot establish this.
Before activation, verify provider configuration, consent, suppression, duplicate
requests, delivery to an owned inbox and correct links. Invitations are sent by
the host from the product, never by the checklist automation.

## Measurement

Existing edition events: checklist_requested, checklist_signup_completed,
checklist_downloaded, checklist_account_clicked. Downloaded currently means a
link click, not proof the file was saved. Signup_completed means lead capture,
not a registered product account. Compare visitors → captured leads → download
clicks → account CTA clicks by edition and acquisition source. Do not use the
existing heuristic account/gathering screen events as confirmed conversions.

After reliable lifecycle tracking is in place, optimize for account activation
and the first successful invitation send, not downloads alone. Review after one
week of actual traffic; low counts are directional, not a conclusive A/B test.
