# How to Publish an Article — The Coordinated Host

You don't need to touch code for any of this.

## 1. Open the editor

Go to **placeandplenty.com/admin** and log in with your Tina Cloud
account (the one you created at app.tina.io).

## 2. Create a new article

Click **The Coordinated Host — Articles** in the sidebar, then
**Create New**.

## 3. Fill in the fields

- **Title, Deck** — the headline and the short line under it.
- **Status** — leave as **draft** while you're writing. Nothing you
  write shows up on the live site until you switch this to
  **published**.
- **Editorial category** — pick one of the six standing categories
  (Beforehand, At My Table, Around the House, People Are Coming,
  HostReady, Plan B).
- **Signature franchise** — only if this fits one of the recurring
  series (How Much?, Do I Actually Need...?, etc.) — otherwise leave
  as "None."
- **Featured image** — click to upload; Tina handles resizing.
- **The Short Answer** — for search-driven articles, put the direct
  answer here (e.g. "You need about 1.5 lbs of ice per guest"). It
  renders as a highlighted callout near the top of the article.
- **Article body** — write here. You can format text, add headings,
  images, and links using the toolbar.
- **SEO title / Meta description** — what shows up in Google search
  results. If left blank, the regular title/deck are used instead.

## 4. Preview

Tina shows a live preview as you type — no need to guess how it'll
look.

## 5. Publish

Change **Status** to **published**, then click **Save**. The article
goes live automatically — Tina commits the change directly to the
website's codebase behind the scenes, and the site rebuilds within a
minute or two.

## 6. Editing later

Come back to `/admin` any time, find the article in the list, make
your changes, and save again.
